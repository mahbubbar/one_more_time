Sports Day is an annual event that brings together students, teachers, and parents to celebrate physical activity and sportsmanship. It is a day filled with excitement, competition, and camaraderie.

Participants engage in various athletic events such as races, relays, and team sports. The event promotes teamwork, discipline, and a healthy lifestyle among students.

Parents and teachers cheer on the participants, creating a lively and supportive atmosphere. Awards and prizes are often given to recognize outstanding performances and sportsmanship.
